# 02 — Operational Threads

## OT-001 — Architecture-to-Cloud Transformation

1. Mission owner approves an outcome and measure.
2. SoS Architecture defines the operational thread, systems, requirements, interfaces, and target-state constraints.
3. Cloud Migration discovers workloads and dependencies and proposes dispositions/waves.
4. Cyber Assurance identifies threats, controls, evidence needs, and authorization dependencies.
5. SoS Architecture adjudicates cross-system conflicts and baselines the coordinated target.
6. Cloud Migration builds and rehearses the target.
7. Cyber Assurance evaluates evidence sufficiency and readiness.
8. Named authorities approve cutover and residual conditions.
9. Migration executes, validates, stabilizes, and exits legacy hosting.
10. Monitoring feeds posture, configuration, cost, and mission results back into architecture governance.

## OT-002 — Material Architecture Change

1. A mission, threat, technology, interface, provider, configuration, cost, or policy change occurs.
2. The authoritative system publishes a signed versioned event.
3. Traceability identifies affected requirements, systems, interfaces, controls, tests, evidence, risks, and decisions.
4. Owners assess impact and alternatives.
5. Architecture governance approves, rejects, or conditions a revised baseline.
6. Cyber and cloud projects implement and verify their allocated changes.
7. Evidence closes the change and monitoring continues.

## OT-003 — Constituent-System Degradation

1. Monitoring detects a constituent-system or shared-service degradation.
2. The owning system publishes status, scope, confidence, and expected duration.
3. SoS Architecture calculates affected mission threads and dependencies.
4. Cyber Assurance updates assurance claims and risk.
5. Cloud Migration/operations invoke approved degraded or recovery behavior.
6. Mission authority selects continue, constrain, recover, or suspend.
7. Recovery evidence restores affected claims.

## Measures

- Time to identify affected mission threads
- Traceability coverage for priority requirements and interfaces
- Interface contract compatibility
- Evidence freshness and confidence
- Time to architecture decision
- Mission-thread success in degraded/recovery conditions
