# 01 — Mission Context and CONOPS

## Mission problem

Complex mission capabilities depend on independently funded, owned, operated, and evolved systems. Without a governing architecture, interfaces drift, dependencies remain hidden, cyber evidence fragments, migration decisions conflict, and local optimization degrades the end-to-end mission.

## Mission outcome

**MO-001:** Enable timely, secure, resilient mission outcomes through coordinated constituent systems, governed interfaces, authoritative data, traceable decisions, and continuous evidence.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Primary concern |
|---|---|
| Mission owner | Operational outcome and consequence |
| Program leadership | Cost, schedule, acquisition, and residual risk |
| Lead systems architect | End-to-end architecture integrity |
| Lead cyber architect | Threats, controls, evidence, and authorization dependencies |
| Cloud/platform architect | Hosting, integration, resilience, and operations |
| Data owner/steward | Authority, quality, lineage, policy, and lifecycle |
| Product/system owners | Constituent capability, delivery, and sustainment |
| Test/assessment | Objective verification and evidence limitations |
| Operators/users | Usability, availability, degraded modes, and recovery |

## CONOPS

1. Define mission outcome, measures, actors, constraints, and priority operational thread.
2. Identify constituent systems, external systems, authorities, boundaries, and dependencies.
3. Derive requirements and allocate functions, services, interfaces, and quality attributes.
4. Cyber Assurance analyzes threats, controls, evidence, and authorization dependencies.
5. Cloud Migration dispositions and migrates eligible constituent workloads.
6. All systems publish versioned state and evidence through governed contracts.
7. Architecture governance evaluates mission, technical, security, cost, schedule, and sustainment tradeoffs.
8. Verification demonstrates the mission thread under normal, degraded, failure, recovery, and change conditions.
9. Monitoring reopens affected claims and decisions when assumptions or configurations change.

## Scope boundaries

The SoS Architecture project owns architectural intent and traceability. It does not execute cloud migration, assess controls independently, or accept residual risk. Those responsibilities remain with the appropriate constituent system and human authority.
