# 08 — Capability Roadmap

| Increment | Outcome | Capabilities | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| C0 — Mobilize | Governed migration program | Mission outcome, owners, inventory model, standards, decision rights | Approved charter and pilot scope |
| C1 — Foundation | Secure landing zone | Organization, accounts, identity, network, logs, keys, backup, IaC | Foundation security/operations review |
| C2 — Pilot | End-to-end migration proof | Discovery, disposition, build, migrate, test, cutover, rollback, retire | Pilot accepted and lessons incorporated |
| C3 — Scale | Repeatable migration waves | Factory workflow, patterns, templates, dependency management | Two waves meet quality and schedule gates |
| C4 — Data and critical systems | High-dependency migration | Replication, reconciliation, resilience, rehearsed recovery | Tier 1/2 cutover and recovery evidence |
| C5 — Modernize | Cloud-native value realization | Managed services, containers, events, APIs, data products | Performance/cost/mission benefit demonstrated |
| C6 — Exit and optimize | Legacy closure and sustainable operations | Decommissioning, FinOps, Well-Architected review, roadmap refresh | Legacy costs closed and operating model accepted |

## Architecture gates

- **Gate A — Mission fit:** outcome, portfolio scope, owners, measures, and constraints accepted.
- **Gate B — Foundation readiness:** landing-zone services meet migration prerequisites.
- **Gate C — Workload readiness:** disposition, dependencies, design, tests, recovery, and operations approved.
- **Gate D — Cutover readiness:** rehearsal, data reconciliation, rollback, staffing, communications, and authority confirmed.
- **Gate E — Stabilization:** SLO, security, cost, support, recovery, and evidence accepted.
- **Gate F — Legacy exit:** source data, interfaces, records, licenses, infrastructure, and financial closure verified.
