# Contributing

Contributions must remain fictional, vendor-neutral, and suitable for unrestricted public release.

1. Open an issue describing the proposed architecture change.
2. Identify the affected mission outcome, requirement, interface, risk, test, and decision records.
3. Update traceability and diagrams with the narrative.
4. Use synthetic identifiers and data only.
5. Confirm that no sensitive operational detail or third-party work product is included.

Architecture decisions that change a boundary, trust assumption, interface contract, control responsibility, or verification claim require a new ADR.
