# Evidence Sufficiency Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 3. A total score supports review prioritization; it never replaces assessor judgment.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Relevance | No claim link | Indirect | Supports part of claim | Directly supports defined claim |
| Provenance | Unknown | Self-asserted | Known source | Authenticated source and lineage |
| Configuration binding | None | General platform | Version identified | Exact representative configuration |
| Integrity | Unverifiable | Transport only | Stored digest | Verified signature/digest and custody |
| Freshness | Expired/unknown | Near/over threshold | Current | Current and event-triggered invalidation |
| Coverage | No scope | Small sample | Representative | Required scope with justified sampling |
| Repeatability | Cannot review | Narrative only | Procedure available | Independent reproduction/review possible |
| Limitations | Hidden | Incomplete | Documented | Quantified and linked to residual risk |

## Suggested interpretation

- **0–8:** insufficient; do not support the assurance claim.
- **9–15:** limited; identify gaps and compensating evidence.
- **16–20:** generally supportable with recorded limitations.
- **21–24:** strong evidence, subject to independent review and context.

## Mandatory stop conditions

Regardless of score, evidence is not acceptable when its source cannot be identified, integrity cannot be established for a high-consequence claim, the tested configuration is materially different without analysis, markings prohibit use, or a known contradiction remains unresolved.
